HOW THE FIGHT WORKS

Fight Paraxis

  • The crest coin decides who goes first: the crest for the crew, the crossed swords for Paraxis.
  • Paraxis has 30 hit points. Rune, Sparrow, Hale, and Scarlett have 4 each.
  • On the crew’s turn, pick each crew member’s move on their card, then roll them all at once. Each one attacks or uses a medpack. An attack rolls a D20, and 15 or higher hits for 1. Hale hits for 2.
  • The crew has 3 medpacks. Each one heals 2 hit points, and it can bring someone back from 0.
  • Paraxis attacks twice a turn. A D4 picks who he swings at, and he hits for 1 if he meets their number: Rune 12, Sparrow 14, Hale 15, Scarlett 11.
  • Anyone who rolls a natural 20 hits twice.
  • Anyone who rolls a 1 takes a hit: the crew member who rolled it loses 1 hit point, and so does Paraxis.
  • Win or lose, the result counts. If more than half the frens beat him, the crew wins. If one of the crew falls in more than half the battles, they fall in the story.
