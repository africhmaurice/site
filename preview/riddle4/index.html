Riddle #4: pick a picture

Each picture would sit on the riddle card itself, so nothing else on the page can change the answer. The question: “How many rectangles can you find in this picture?” Every box counts, and so does every bigger rectangle made from boxes that touch.

A · Cabin window

2 panes wide × 3 tall · easiest

Answer: 18

Columns: 1+2 = 3 ways · rows: 1+2+3 = 6 ways · 3 × 6 = 18

B · Cargo crate

3 × 3 panels · medium

Answer: 36

Columns: 1+2+3 = 6 ways · rows: 6 ways · 6 × 6 = 36

C · Treasure map grid ✓ chosen

4 squares wide × 3 tall · hard

Answer: 60

Columns: 1+2+3+4 = 10 ways · rows: 6 ways · 10 × 6 = 60

Rough sketches to pick a direction. The final art would match the riddle cards and could be drawn in your own style. The dotted path and ✕ on the map aren’t boxes and don’t count.
