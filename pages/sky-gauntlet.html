How it works

Your airship

  • Your cannons never stop firing.Fly anywhere in the lower part of the sky: arrows or WASD, or follow with the mouse or your finger. P or Esc pauses. You start with 3 lives.

Weapon level and hull

  • Mark of the CrestEvery crest you catch counts toward two upgrades that last the whole run. The top-left corner shows both, and how many crests you need for the next one.

Weapon level. More crests, better missiles:

  • Level 1Start. A thin bolt.
  • Level 22 crests. Chevrons, faster.
  • Level 34 crests. Fire darts, twice the damage.
  • Level 48 crests. Spinners, twice the damage, and they go through a ship.
  • Level 516 crests. A beam that burns whatever is above you.

Hull durability. Crests toughen your hull too, at twice the count: with 4 crests each life takes 2 hits, with 8 it takes 3, with 16 it takes 4, and it keeps doubling from there. Each hit costs one point of hull, the last one costs a life, and your hull refills for the next life.

Grab these

  • Golden chest+25 hunt points on the leaderboard, up to 4 a day.

Specials, from wave 3

  • ScytheA red slash sweeps up the sky and cuts down what it touches.
  • Wave of energyA ring of energy rings out from your ship: 2 hits to every ship it passes, and it clears the cannonballs it touches.
  • Red atomic3 hits to every ship on screen, and every cannonball is gone.

Dodge these

  • Rival shipsThey loop around, swoop down at you, and drop cannonballs. 100 points a hit to sink one, 200 if it is diving.
  • Teal: 2 hits, wave 3+
  • Green: 3 hits, wave 10+
  • Parchment: 4 hits, wave 15+
  • Ink: 5 hits, wave 20+
  • Gold: 10 hits, wave 25+
  • GunboatCrosses from the side, firing darts at you. 300 points.
  • DartDives straight at you from above, and from below too. 150 points.
  • Mine-layerCrosses the top, dropping mines. Shoot the mines too. 400 points, 25 a mine.
  • FreighterSlow and tough, 8 hits, and it bursts into 4 rubies. 800 points.
  • DestroyerEvery 5th wave. A boss with a hull bar that fires fans of darts. 5,000 points, and it drops a crest.
  • Lightning and windFrom wave 6, a flashing stripe warns where lightning will strike. From wave 8, gusts of wind push your ship.

Tough ships get angry at half hull: faster, with a Scarlet glow.

Scoring and the loot box

Every ship you sink adds its points, and clearing a wave adds 250 times the wave number. Survive 25 waves and loot box 78 floats down. Fly into it to claim it. The top score at 11:59 PM ET every Friday wins 500 hunt points.
