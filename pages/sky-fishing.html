Upgrades

Coins: 0

Drag on the sky to aim: the farther you drag, the harder the shot. The dots show the start of the arc, and the wind does the rest. Walk and jump before you fire. The view follows the action: drag with two fingers (or with the right mouse button) to look around, tap the small map in the corner to jump there, and use Overview to see the whole map. On a keyboard: A and D or the left and right arrows walk, W or J jumps, up and down arrows aim, Q and E set the power, 1 to 5 pick a weapon, N switches crew, and Space fires.

  • Mortar Stone: unlimited, steady.
  • Lightning Strike: lightning strikes the top of the spot you hit.
  • Red Atomic: one huge blast.
  • Barrage: fire a flare and an airship drops a string of bombs around it.
  • Every third turn, a random drop lands on each side of the map: health, gems, extra turns, extra lives, big boulders, coins, weapons, or upgrades. Walk onto one to grab it.
  • Goma Bomb: splits into three stones in the air (unlock it in the upgrades).
  • Coins: +50 at the start of each of your turns, 100 for every coin you grab, 10 for a hit, and 50 for a knockout. Spend them on upgrades: between levels in Campaign, and on your turn in Free-for-All (tap Upgrades).
  • Water sinks a goon, and lava burns one up. Either way, it is out.
  • Stars: 1 for winning a level, 2 for winning with at least two goons standing, and 3 for winning with every goon standing.
  • Your Campaign run saves between levels on this device. A loss ends the run.
