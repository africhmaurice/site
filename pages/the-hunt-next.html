A TRANSMISSION FROM THE CAPTAIN
INCOMING TRANSMISSION - SHOT DOWN OVER DE’GATÉ
0:00

The Second Wind crosses the Gresnick Ocean and finally reaches De’gaté, the Gate of Death. The new captain takes the helm, unrolls the treasure map, and the crew flies recon over the island: past the Well, over the Cathedral, down to the Sword, and on to the Goma Skull.

At the skull, the alarms go off. Missile defense systems hidden in the mountains lock on, and a robotic voice tells them they are not welcome here. The Second Wind takes a hit to the port engine. The shields flicker and give out, and the captain fights the stick all the way down.

The ship comes down hard in the gomas’ valley, half a kilometer south of the skull. The whole crew makes it. The Second Wind doesn’t. Now the crew needs a plan, and the frens decide what it is.
