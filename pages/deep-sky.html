The Deep Sky

Ten constellations hide in this sky, and their stars look like every other star. Open one in the list to study its shape, then trace it: tap its stars in order to draw its lines, and tap Lift to start a new line. Drag a star you tapped onto another star to move it, and press Ctrl+Z to undo. Drag to move around the sky, and zoom in to tell close stars apart. Chart all ten to find the secret one, and the loot box inside it is worth 1,000 points. Each constellation’s first three stars are marked and numbered in the sky, with its name beside star 1. Trace at least 80% of its stars and 80% of its lines to chart it.
