How it plays

  • Crates fall from the open sky with nothing holding them. Move and turn each one to put it in the right place.
  • Fill a whole row and an airship lifts that row away. Only full rows ship.
  • Whatever is left settles down, and that can ship more cargo.
  • Every 3 rows you ship is a new level, and every 3 levels the crates fall faster. If the stack hits the sky, the game is over.
  • Play as often as you like. Your best score this week goes on the weekly board.
