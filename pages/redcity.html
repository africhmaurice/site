STEP ONE

Recruit the crew

Pick three jobs you want on the crew. The five most wanted across all the frens make the crew.

STEP TWO

Work the Pontune market

Seven kits, each packed for the whole crew and priced in credits. You can pick 3 kits. The kits with the most votes will be purchased until you run out of credits.

STEP THREE

The plan

Three decisions for the island. Two of them are encounters: everyone rolls a d20, and the crew's average roll decides how the chosen option plays out. There are no wrong answers, only consequences.

About the dice: the rolling die is a visualization. Your actual roll is made by a random number generator on my server the moment you press Roll. It is recorded straight away and can't be changed or rerolled, and the die simply shows the number it gave.
