STEP ONE

Recruit the crew

Pick four recruits. Each one brings their own kit, and their credits go into the crew purse. Each job also gives the whole crew a perk in Act Two.

Crew rule: only one job per crew. Once you pick a Gunslinger, the other Gunslingers are off the table.

STEP TWO

Work the Pontune market

Spend the purse. There isn't enough for everything, so choose carefully. Items marked behind the counter unlock only if the purse grows before voting closes.

Weapons only help if a recruit can wield them, so check your crew's weapon types.
